\begin{abstract}
Transparent fault tolerance---making an \emph{unmodified} server crash-recoverable
with no code changes---has repeatedly failed to reach production, not because
snapshotting is hard but because of three coupled costs that were all large at the
millisecond operating point every prior system lived at: logging
non-determinism (above all message order), coordinating a distributed consistent
cut, and holding externally-visible output until its producing state is durable.
The industry instead rewrote applications to externalize state---durable execution,
exactly-once stream processing---winning mindshare but leaving the legacy fleet
behind. We revisit the abandoned transparent path and show it becomes practical
under two co-designed shifts. First, a user-space \emph{determinism libOS}
(\texttt{LD\_PRELOAD}, no kernel or application changes) makes unmodified servers
deterministically recoverable by virtualizing their residual local
non-determinism---time, randomness, and thread scheduling---at a few-percent
steady-state overhead; we demonstrate byte-identical recovery of stock Redis,
Memcached, Nginx, Node.js, and a multi-process PostgreSQL. Second, routing those
servers through a microsecond-scale in-network total-order reliable fabric
(1Pipe) collapses all three coupled costs: the fabric \emph{is} the order, so the
replay order-log vanishes; a timestamp-$T$ snapshot replaces the Chandy--Lamport
cut; and the output-commit barrier \emph{coincides} with the fabric's two-phase
reliable-delivery commit barrier, so the durable-replica write rides under a
barrier the system already pays for and fault tolerance adds $\approx$0 latency
over the reliable-fabric baseline. We quantify the overlap (0.23\% marginal cost
in reproduction; corroborated by real RDMA verbs at $\sim$1.5\,\textmu s over
SoftRoCE), verify exactly-once and linearizability under injected crashes,
machine-check the core protocols in TLA+, and position the result honestly against
LLFT and HyCoR, which substantially anticipate the idea at the millisecond scale.
The contribution is a realization-and-measurement: at the in-network-total-order,
microsecond operating point, a long-dismissed technique becomes a byproduct of the
communication fabric.
\end{abstract}
